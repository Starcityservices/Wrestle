Single-Leg Finish

Attack → shelf → run the pipe.

Cue: get your head up, keep your knee tight, and turn the corner before you try to finish.
🛡️

Sprawl & Go-Behind

Stop the shot and score immediately.

Cue: hips heavy, chest pressure, then circle behind instead of standing straight up.
🤼

Hand Fight

Win inside position first.

Cue: forehead position, inside ties, short snaps, and constant foot movement.
↘️

Double-Leg

Level change before penetration.

Cue: change levels with your legs, step between their feet, drive through the opponent.
🔒

Top Breakdown

Pressure before the turn.

Cue: make them carry your weight, flatten the hips, then attack the near side.
🔄

Stand-Up

Hands first, hips second.

Cue: protect your hands, build your base, clear the hands, then explode to your feet.
